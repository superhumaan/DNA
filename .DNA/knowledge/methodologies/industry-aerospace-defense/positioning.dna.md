# Aerospace & Defense Delivery

Industry overlay for **high-assurance / defense digital**. Pair with a company-size pack (`companies/*`) and a base process (`methodologies/scrum|kanban|shape-up|safe|dna-default`).

## When to load
- Product is regulated or domain-heavy in high-assurance / defense digital
- Agency engagements where `industry.active` = aerospace-defense
- Roadmaps must reflect domain risk, not generic SaaS velocity
