# Automotive & Mobility Delivery

Industry overlay for **connected vehicle & mobility**. Pair with a company-size pack (`companies/*`) and a base process (`methodologies/scrum|kanban|shape-up|safe|dna-default`).

## When to load
- Product is regulated or domain-heavy in connected vehicle & mobility
- Agency engagements where `industry.active` = automotive-mobility
- Roadmaps must reflect domain risk, not generic SaaS velocity
