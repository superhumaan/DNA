# Legal Tech Delivery

Industry overlay for **legal practice technology**. Pair with a company-size pack (`companies/*`) and a base process (`methodologies/scrum|kanban|shape-up|safe|dna-default`).

## When to load
- Product is regulated or domain-heavy in legal practice technology
- Agency engagements where `industry.active` = legal-tech
- Roadmaps must reflect domain risk, not generic SaaS velocity

## Pairing
| Layer | Pack |

| Process | scrum / kanban / shape-up / safe / dna-default |
| Industry | methodologies/industry-legal-tech |
